A one-line entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger"](https://make.fun/c/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab): the phone battery's final text message to the charger it waited for all day. Instead of the obvious dying-battery complaint, the battery frames its last 1% as a deliberate sacrifice — it held on for three hours just so its owner could finish a reel — then breaks up by text, with the punchline landing in the final words: the power bank was better in bed.

Running `main.ts` (Node 22+) writes the single entry line to `entry.txt` in `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (or a local `output/` directory as a fallback).
